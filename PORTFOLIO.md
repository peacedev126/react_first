## Testing & Quality Engineering

My testing work is focused on finding real problems, not just increasing test counts. I have written automated tests, investigated failures through logs and application flows, validated data, reviewed code, and helped teams improve software quality before production releases.

When a test fails, I work through the evidence to understand whether the cause is a product defect, a test issue, bad data, timing, or the environment. I prefer fixing the root cause and adding useful regression coverage rather than repeatedly patching the same symptom.

I am also comfortable combining automated testing with manual validation when a feature or workflow needs direct verification.
